How the diagram reads

You start at na facing ga (the bottom of the page). Figures on the left and right are seen from the side; centre figures face you going toward ga and show their back returning toward na. Horse- and crane-stance figures face the way the body faces.

Floor line

The path of the form on its {{GLYPH}} line, one count at a time: the red line is where you have been, the large dot is where you stand now and its tick points the way you face.

Footwork

Every footprint, labelled with the count that puts the foot there. Counts made without a step (a block then a punch) are added to the label of the feet you stand on, e.g. “5 6”. Dashed arrows are steps; a ring marks a foot that pivots in place. The selected count is red and the outlined pair is where you stand after it. Positions are worked out from the stances and turns, so distances are approximate.
