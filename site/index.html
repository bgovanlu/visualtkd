About

What this is

visualtkd is a study aid for the official taekwondo forms: the Kukkiwon poomsae of World Taekwondo now, and the ITF patterns next. The old book diagrams are hard to read on a phone and harder to teach from; these keep the original floor lines but redraw every move with a consistent figure, footprints and plain instructions.

It is for students learning a form, and for instructors who want a clean sheet to hand out.

Sources

Sequences are checked against the Kukkiwon page for each form and at least one published move chart. Where sources disagree, or no source settles a detail, the choice is written down for review rather than decided silently.

Every form page links to its official references. visualtkd is independent and is not affiliated with Kukkiwon, World Taekwondo or any ITF federation.

Print sheets and QR codes

Each form prints as two US Letter pages: the floor plan, then the full sequence and references. A QR code on each page opens the form on your phone, and every reference has its own short visualtkd.com/go/ link, so printed sheets keep working even when a video or page moves.

World Taekwondo form names, sequences and counts follow Kukkiwon; ITF patterns follow the ITF Encyclopedia of Taekwon-Do. Descriptions are our own words; see each form’s official references for the source text.
