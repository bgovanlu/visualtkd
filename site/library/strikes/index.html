Momtong jireugi 몸통지르기

Middle punch

ITF Kaunde jirugi 가운데 지르기 · Middle punch

A straight punch to the solar plexus. The fist turns palm-down as it lands while the other fist pulls back to the hip. Called bandae when the punching hand matches the front foot, baro when it is the opposite hand.
