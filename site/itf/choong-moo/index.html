I · Choong-Moo · the admiral

Choong-Moo is the title given to Admiral Yi Sun-sin of the Joseon (Yi) dynasty, credited with the armoured turtle ship (geobukseon) of 1592. The pattern ends with a left-hand attack for his early death, before he could show his full ability. The diagram is I. You start in parallel ready stance in the middle of the A–B line facing D: knife-hand blocks and strikes and a fingertip thrust to B and A; down the stem, a side kick back toward C and a flying side kick toward D; on the E–F line, a grab and knee kick, a reverse knife-hand strike, turning and back kicks; back up toward C, a U-shape block, a jumping spin, fingertip thrusts and a back fist; then to B and A, a double forearm block, a block and back fist in sitting stance, two side kicks, an X-knife-hand checking block, a twin palm block, a rising block and a final left punch.

How the diagram reads

You start at na facing ga (the bottom of the page). Figures on the left and right are seen from the side; centre figures face you going toward ga and show their back returning toward na. Horse- and crane-stance figures face the way the body faces.

Floor line

The path of the form on its I line, one count at a time: the red line is where you have been, the large dot is where you stand now and its tick points the way you face.

Footwork

Every footprint, labelled with the count that puts the foot there. Counts made without a step (a block then a punch) are added to the label of the feet you stand on, e.g. “5 6”. Dashed arrows are steps; a ring marks a foot that pivots in place. The selected count is red and the outlined pair is where you stand after it. Positions are worked out from the stances and turns, so distances are approximate.

Move numbers follow the ITF Encyclopedia count (30 movements; the flying side kick and the guarding block on landing in 9, and the front block and back fist in 24, are split into -1 and -2 here). Directions use the ITF letters: you start facing D, with B on your left and A on your right; E and F are the ends of the far line, DE and DF the diagonals the turning kicks go toward. Finish by bringing the left foot back to parallel ready stance.
