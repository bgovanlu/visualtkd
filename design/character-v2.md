# Character v2: references, brief and 10 model directions (15 October 2026)

## Why v2
Brian's notes on the A1 "Natural" figure (current renderer):
- **Ap kubi:** the rear leg must be nearly straight. The current figure bends it.
- **Dwit kubi:** the rear knee points out to the side, and the hips and chest turn to the side. The current figure keeps the body square to the front foot.
- The ears and fists are ugly.
- You can't see where the feet are.
- First step: a character that can actually be posed this way (hip rotation separate from foot direction, visible feet, knee direction under control).

## What the new figure must do (brief used for every model image)
1. Realistic adult proportions, about 7.5 heads; athletic; gender-neutral; faceless; **no ears** (or a minimal curve).
2. **Hips and chest rotate independently of the feet**: dwit kubi turns the torso about 90°; ap kubi keeps it square.
3. **Knee direction is its own control**: forward over the toes (ap kubi front leg), out to the side (dwit kubi rear leg, horse stance).
4. **Rear leg straightens** in ap kubi; the heel stays down and the foot turns out about 30°.
5. **Feet always readable**: heel, arch and toes, never hidden behind a shin or the far leg. In side view the far foot shows past the near one.
6. **Fists**: compact, rounded, a visible knuckle line and a wrapped thumb; one shape per view (side, front and back).
7. Near limbs light, far limbs darker (kept from v1: this is how left and right are read).
8. Rig-friendly: segments for upper arm, forearm, hand, thigh, shin, foot, pelvis, chest and head, with joint circles hidden under the cloth.

The full prompts are stored with each job in Higgsfield (job IDs above). Image models often get stances wrong, so judge each sheet's **style** separately from its **stance accuracy**.

## How to choose (check each sheet)
- [ ] Pose 4: rear leg nearly straight, heel down, foot turned out
- [ ] Pose 5: torso sideways, rear knee out, heels on one line
- [ ] Pose 6: knees pushed out over the toes
- [ ] Feet readable in every pose
- [ ] Fists clean in side and front view
- [ ] Still reads at about 110 px tall (the board card size)
