Character sheet

Five designs for the Taegeuk diagrams, each in the same six poses: ready, side punch in both directions, front stance seen from the front, front kick, and the back view. Pick a letter and it replaces the figure across every form.

Belt
All five share one skeleton, so any of them can draw every pose in all eight Taegeuk forms. Proportions, line weight and colours can still be tuned after you choose.
