# visualtkd.com — working conventions

Modern diagrams of the Kukkiwon poomsae (Taegeuk 1–8 and the black-belt forms): one page per form, a two-page print sheet per form, a forms index, a landing page, a how-to-read guide and a technique library. Read this before adding or changing a form.

## How a form is built

Every page is **generated**; never hand-edit anything under `site/`.

- `templates/form.html` — the form page template (layout, board, carousel, study panel, table, print hooks). Change shared form-page behaviour here.
- `templates/figure.js` — the figure renderer, footprints and stance chips (`skel()`, `draw()`, `feet()`, `chip()`), inlined into form pages and the library/how-to-read pages.
- `templates/footwork.js` — the floor simulator (both feet after every count, from stance, lead, facing and the instruction text) and the footwork glyph and whole-form plan, shown with `?footwork`.
- `templates/base.css` — colour tokens, type, site nav, footer and chip styles shared by every page.
- `data/techniques.json` — the technique library: names (romanised, Hangul, English), family, surface, height and description for every `tech` and `stance` key. The form pages read their labels from here.
- `data/catalog.json` — every form the site will cover, in teaching order; a form is published once its `forms/*.json` exists. Two families: **World Taekwondo forms** (`taegeuk` + `blackbelt`, the Kukkiwon poomsae) and **ITF forms** (`itf`, the 24 Chang Hon patterns, planned). Each `itf` entry carries its `diagram`: the Encyclopedia floor line as an SVG path on a 40×40 grid, with D (the way the student faces at the start) at the top, C at the bottom, B left and A right, and `start` = the ready position. The forms index draws it on the pattern's card.
- `forms/<form>.json` — everything specific to one form: moves, floor-plan layout, minimap positions, trigram (Taegeuk) or Hanja `symbol` (black-belt forms), optional `floor` shape, notes, references.
- `tools/build_form.py` — renders template + data into `site/<slug>/index.html`, builds the print PDF with `--print`, decodes every QR in that PDF, regenerates `site/_redirects`, then runs `build_site.py`.
- `design/moves/<form>/` — per-move figure images (#7): `SPEC.md` is the image brief per count (generated by `tools/move_specs.py`); `<id>.png` images dropped there are cut out by `tools/figures.py` and used instead of the vector figure with `build_form.py --images` (builds into `preview/`, never deployed). See `design/moves/README.md`.
- `tools/build_site.py` — the other pages: `/` (landing + About), `/forms/` (two sections: World Taekwondo forms and ITF forms, each with its official references from `wtRefs` / `itfRefs` in the catalog; each ITF card links to its pattern page via `url`), `/history/` (a short history of taekwondo, the kwans, the ITF, the Kukkiwon and WTF → World Taekwondo, with official references), `/how-to-read/`, `/library/` and `/library/<family>/`. Reference cards on these pages use `sitekit.ref_card` and `REF_CSS`, with the same icons as the form pages. "Appears in" lists and counts are computed from the forms.

```
python3 tools/build_form.py forms/taegeuk-2.json --print     # one form
python3 tools/build_form.py --all --print                     # everything (after a template change)
```

Setup: `cd tools && npm install && pip install -r requirements.txt && python -m playwright install chromium`. The PDF needs `pdftoppm` (poppler) and the system font Noto Serif CJK KR.

A new form is picked up automatically: once `forms/<form>.json` exists for a slug in `data/catalog.json`, its card opens, the progress meter moves and the library lists its moves. Check the pages with `tools/check/pages.py` (desktop + phone screenshots through a local server; reports script errors and phone overflow).

## Review list

`REVIEW.md` lists every source disagreement and unsettled choice, per form. When you add or change a form, add its open items there (and in the commit message).

## Adding a form — checklist

1. **Verify the sequence against at least two sources** before writing data: the Kukkiwon page for the form plus a published move chart. Check stance (ap seogi vs ap kubi, which foot leads), technique, hand/foot, direction, and kihap for every move. The taekwondo.fandom.com wiki may be used as a cross-check source, never as a page link. Any disagreement between sources, or any choice not settled by a source, is listed for Brian's review in the commit/PR, not decided silently.
2. Write `forms/<form>.json` (copy the nearest existing form).
3. Build with `--print`; the QR check must pass.
4. Look at the desktop floor plan, a phone carousel card and both PDF pages before committing (`tools/check/shot.py <slug> [phone]`, `tools/check/figs.py` for new poses).
5. Update the index card, commit, push.

## Move data (`moves[]`)

```
{"id":"8-2","dir":"da2","view":"side","face":"R","stance":"front","lead":"R",
 "arms":{"R":"hpunch"},"tech":"hpunch","hand":"R","note":"bandae","desc":"…"}
```

- `id`: Kukkiwon counting. A kick and the strike after it share a number: `8-1` (kick), `8-2` (strike). Ready is `J`.
- `dir`: `na` (top/start), `ga` (bottom), `da1–3` (right side of page), `ra1–3` (left side), and the diagonals `gada`, `nada`, `gara`, `nara` (Hansu; side view, facing R for da diagonals, L for ra). You start at na facing ga, so a **left** turn from the start faces **da** (right of the page).
- `view`: `side` for moves along the ra/da bars (needs `face`: `R` = facing right of page, `L` = left); `front` for moves toward ga; `back` for moves toward na. Exception: side and roundhouse kicks (`skick`, `rkick`) are always drawn in profile. On the centre line, set `face` so the kicking leg is the near (white) leg; on the side bars, `face` follows the direction of travel.
- `stance`: `ready`, `close` (moa seogi), `walk` (ap seogi), `front` (ap kubi), `back` (dwit kubi), `tiger` (beom seogi), `horse` (juchum seogi), `cross` (kkoa seogi), `crane` (hakdari seogi; `lead` = the **standing** leg), `nieun` (nieunja seogi, ㄴ-shaped stance), `gyeot` (gyeotdari seogi, assisting stance), `ogeum` (ogeum seogi, reverse crane stance; `lead` = the standing leg), `kick` (ap chagi, also used for jumping and target kicks), `skick` (yeop chagi), `rkick` (dollyeo chagi), `knee` (mureup chigi). `lead` = forward foot, or the kicking leg for kicks. For `back`, `lead` is the **front** foot; descriptions say "back stance with the left foot forward" so the naming convention can't be misread.
- `arms`: per hand. Fist: `low`, `punch`, `hpunch`, `inside`, `outer`, `high`, `backfist`, `guard`, `junbi`. Open hand (drawn flat): `ksout` (single knife-hand outer block), `ksblock` + `ksguard` (double knife-hand block: blocking hand + guarding hand), `spear` + `press` (spear-hand thrust + pressing hand under the elbow), `neck` (knife-hand neck strike), `khigh` (knife-hand high block; with `neck` = swallow-form), `twist` (twisted knife-hand high block), `palm` (palm inside block), `kslow` (low knife-hand block), `ksside` (knife-hand side block), `eltarget` / `grab` (open target or pulling hand). More fists: `hammer`, `elbow`, `houter` (high outer block), `spread`, `xlow` (low X block), `wedge`, `uppercut`, `cover` (covered fist), `sidepunch`, `assist` (supporting fist under the blocking arm). Combined techniques are built per hand: scissors = `outer` + `low`, mountain = `houter` + `low`. Unlisted hands are chambered at the hip.
  Black-belt forms add: `tongmil` (pushing-hands ready), `ksstrike` (knife-hand outer strike), `arc` (kaljaebi), `kneebreak` + `hold`, `target` + `crosspunch` (target punch), `lspear` + `shoulder` (low spear hand, palm up), `pressdown` (palm pressing block), `sideelbow` + `elbowpush`, `hammerlow` + `palmlow`, `palmjaw`, `ksin` (knife-hand inside block), `hingepunch` + `sideelbow` (large hinge), `hingetop` (small hinge, other fist chambered), `hammerout`, `kspread`, `tgrab` (twisting grab). Diamond block = `high` + `low`; diamond middle block = `outer` + `high`; santeul (mountain) = `houter` + `houter`.
  Strikes that come straight at the viewer in front view are listed in `TOWARD` in the template and drawn foreshortened.
- `tech` keys the technique library `data/techniques.json` (every key above plus combined names such as `scissors`, `mountain`, `duppercut`, `apalm`, `aouter`, `alow`, `obackfist`, `tkick`, `jkick`). A new technique needs an entry there (family, slug, names, surface, height, a description in our own words); **the build fails if a move uses a technique or stance with no entry.**
- Steps with several techniques (double punch, block then punch) get sub-numbers like kicks: `17-1` block, `17-2` punch, `17-3` punch. One figure per count.
- `note`: `bandae` (punch hand = lead foot) or `baro` (opposite hand). `kihap: true` on the shouting move.
- `carry: false` — the stance matches the count before but the feet move (a cross-step, a 360° turn); stops the same-stance treatment (#4).
- `step` — footwork simulator hint (#3), only where the instruction text isn't enough: `{"plant": "L"|"R"|"both"|"none", "move": [forward, right], "horse": "along"}`. Check with `tools/check/footwork.py` (every form should end within a stride or two of where it started).
- `desc`: one plain instruction sentence: turn and direction, stance, technique.

## ITF forms (`"family": "itf"`)

- Set `"family": "itf"` in the form JSON. The page then uses the ITF names (`itf` in `data/techniques.json` entries, via `sitekit.lib_js(itf=True)`), the ITF direction letters and the ITF legend and reading note.
- **Directions:** the site keeps its own orientation: you start facing down the page. So `ga` = **D**, `na` = **C**, `da1`/`da2` = **B** (the student's left, right of the page), `ra1`/`ra2` = **A**, and on the far bar `ra3` = **E** and `da3` = **F** (Dan-Gun's I). `floor.letters` overrides the letters per row, e.g. `{"r5": ["E", "F"]}` (left, right of the page).
- **ITF arm poses:** `sideblk` (inner forearm side block, forearm out in front of the shoulder, clear of the face in profile) and `twinhigh` (the rising arm of the twin forearm block). Twin forearm block = `sideblk` + `twinhigh`. `xshoulder` = fist drawn back in front of the opposite shoulder (Won-Hyo's inward strike). `bfside` = back fist high side strike (in front view the fist is foreshortened beside the head). Diagonals: `nara` = **CE**, `nada` = **CF**, `gara` = **DE**, `gada` = **DF** (from the r5 letters). `floor.diag` renames them per form, e.g. Yul-Gok `{"gara": "AD", "gada": "BD"}`. `kstwinhigh` = the open-hand rising arm of the twin knife-hand block. Joong-Gun adds `twinv` (twin vertical punch), `upset` (upset punch), `uhigh`/`ulow` (U-shape block) and `palmup` (upward palm block); front-view `hpunch` is foreshortened with the fist at chin height. Toi-Gye adds `bfback` (back fist side back strike, the fist beside the head). Hwa-Rang adds `ksdown` (knife-hand downward strike, open hand brought down to chest height). Choong-Moo adds `kxcheck` (X-knife-hand checking block, hands crossed in front of the chest).  Kwang-Gae adds `heaven` (heaven hand, both open hands raised in front of the forehead). Spokes can end at a side group: `{"from": "r5", "to": "c4l"}`. The ITF books draw D at the top, seen from behind; the reading note says so.
- **Stances:** walking stance (gunnun sogi) uses `front`; L-stance (niunja sogi) uses `back`; fixed stance (gojung sogi) is its own key `fixed`, drawn and simulated as `back` but chip-named by the front foot; bending ready stance (guburyo sogi) uses `crane` (`lead` = the standing leg); sitting stance (annun sogi) uses `horse`; rear foot stance (dwitbal sogi) uses `tiger`, chip-named by the rear foot; low stance (nachuo sogi) is `lowst`, drawn and simulated as `front`; X-stance (kyocha sogi) uses `cross`. One-leg stance (waebal sogi) is `oneleg`, drawn and simulated as `crane` (`lead` = the standing leg). Vertical stance (soojik sogi) is `vertical`, drawn and simulated as `back` and chip-named by the **rear** foot like the L-stance. Chips and instructions name the L-stance by the **rear** foot, as the ITF does (`lead` stays the front foot in the data).
- **Notes:** punches use `note: "obverse"` / `"reverse"` (the English words, because ITF baro/bandae run the other way from Kukkiwon).
- **Counting and references:** move ids follow the ITF Encyclopedia count. `refs` use kind `itf` (red gate icon); short links `itf-<name>` and `itf-encyclopedia`.

## Floor plan (`layout`, `pos`, `boardMin`)

- Keep the original book layout: the 王 shape. `r1`, `r3`, `r5` are the three bars (left → right on the page); `c2`, `c4` are the centre column between bars. Group a kick with its strike, and figures that share a spot, in a nested list: `["16-2","16-1"]`.
- Every move id must appear exactly once in `layout` and in `pos` (build fails otherwise).
- `pos` is the phone minimap position on a 100×60 grid: bars at y 8/30/52, centre x 50, ra side x 10–30, da side x 70–90.
- Other floor lines (black-belt forms) use `floor` instead of changing the template: `glyph` (header, e.g. 士), `bars` per row (`full`, `fit` = line only under its figures, `noline` = no bar, figures centred on the stem, `ends` = no bar, groups at both ends, `fit hookl`/`fit hookr` = short bar from the centre line out to one side, `gone` = row removed), `rail` (rows joined by the centre line, e.g. `["r1","c4"]`), `prongs` (extra vertical lines up or down to side groups `c2l`/`c2r`/`c4l`/`c4r`), `spokes` (diagonal lines from the centre of one row to a group in another, `{from:"r3",to:"r1",g:0}`), `notesBelow` (moves the four side notes under the board so middle rows can hold figures), `spots` (one-line floors: `layout.r1` is a list of spots on the line from ra to da, each drawn as a column of the counts made there; every other row is removed) and `map` (minimap path). Na stays at the top, so the glyph is drawn upside down: Koryo 士 (r1 fit, r3 long bar, stem to c4), Keumgang 山 (r1 base, prongs in c2l/c2r), Taebaek 工 (r1 and r5 bars), Pyongwon 一 (`spots`: one line through the start, five spots from the ra end to the da end), Sipjin 十 (r3 full bar through the start, r1 `noline` stem end toward na, rail r1→c4 with the ga arm in c4), Jitae ㅗ (r1 full base line through the start, stem down through c2, r3 `noline`, c4), Cheonkwon ㅜ drawn as ㅗ (r1 `noline` stem end with the start, stem rows c2/r3/c4, r5 the bar), Hansu 水 (r3 `noline` centre line, r1/r5 `ends` = no bar with the corner groups at both ends, `spokes` from r3 to each corner group, returns in c2l/c2r/c4l/c4r), Ilyeo 卍 (r3 full bar, r1 `fit hookl` and r5 `fit hookr` short bars from the centre line, prongs up to c2r and down to c4l).
- On a bar, horse- and crane-stance figures face the way the body faces (usually ga, front view); the technique goes to the side.
- `boardMin` = desktop board width in px; raise it when a bar holds more figures (Il Jang 1120, Ee Jang 1320).
- Optional `cardW` / `figH` shrink the figure cards when a bar holds 9+ figures (Sam Jang 104/124, Sa Jang 112/132). Keep the print sheet to exactly 2 pages; check the page count after building.

## Figure

- Character v2 (#7) is generated one image per distinct pose from a 3D pose guide: `tools/poses/` (`inventory.py` → `render.py` → `cutouts.py` → `pair.py`), kit in `design/models/v4/` (see its README). A new form adds poses: rerun the four steps.

- Design **Textbook line A1 "Natural"**: realistic adult proportions, single-contour limbs, sloped shoulders, faceless head (profile shape only on side views), short hair cap above the hairline, black-collar dobok, belt in the selected colour.
- **Near limbs white, far limbs grey** — this is how left/right is read; keep it for every pose.
- Seen from behind, a kicking leg is raised in front of the body: draw it over the torso in the far-side grey. (Brian finds this weak; redesign tracked in GitHub issue #1.)
- Seen from the front, punches and knife-hand neck strikes come toward the viewer: draw the arm foreshortened with the fist face / knife-hand edge, never across the face.
- New poses go in the template's `skel()`; check them in front, side and back views before use.
- No faces, no brand logos, no copyrighted characters.

## Stance cues

- Chips: walking stance = outlined, front stance = **filled**, back stance = double outline, kick = dashed, crane = dotted blue.
- Footprint glyph (top view, rotated to the facing direction): red foot leads, blue tick = facing. Ap seogi short and nearly in line; ap kubi long and fist-width wide with the rear foot turned out; dwit kubi an L shape, heels in line, rear foot turned 90°.

## URLs — stable, because they are printed

- ITF patterns: `/itf/<name>`, e.g. `/itf/chon-ji` (published, printed), `/itf/se-jong`. Form files are `forms/itf-<name>.json`.
- Form pages: `visualtkd.com/taegeuk/1` … `/taegeuk/8`; black-belt forms by name: `/koryo`, `/keumgang`, `/taebaek` `/pyongwon`, `/sipjin`, `/jitae`, `/cheonkwon`, `/hansu`, `/ilyeo`. **Never rename a published path**; printed QR codes point there.
- References never link out directly from paper. Each has a short link `visualtkd.com/go/<name>` (e.g. `kukkiwon-t2`, `video-t2`) defined by the form's `refs[].go`; `site/_redirects` is generated from all forms as 302 redirects. If a destination moves, fix the JSON `url` and rebuild — never reprint.
- One `go` name must always mean one URL (the build fails on conflicts).
- Library: `/library/<family>/#<slug>` with families `stances`, `blocks`, `strikes`, `kicks`, `hands-feet`. Slugs are the romanised name (`ap-kubi`, `arae-makki`, `sonnal`) and must never change once published. Moves deep-link as `/<form>/#move-<id>` (e.g. `/taegeuk/8/#move-19-2`).
- `/` is the landing page, `/forms/` the forms index and `/history/` the history page; printed QR codes only use form paths and `/go/`.

## References

Only official sources, in this order: the Kukkiwon page for the form, the Kukkiwon poomsae index, the Kukkiwon Digital Encyclopedia video for the form (published on YouTube by Mookas TV; say so), the Kukkiwon YouTube channel, the World Taekwondo Poomsae Competition Rules PDF. Each reference card has a generic icon (gate = Kukkiwon, play = video, channel, document = PDF) and a source label; do not draw brand logos.

## Print sheet (US Letter, 2 pages)

- Page 1: header + full floor plan; the belt picker becomes a ruled "Practice notes" box.
- Page 2: full sequence table + references.
- Long forms: when the board forces page 1 below ~0.49 scale, `build_print.py` tightens the table rows and zooms page 2 back up as far as it still fits on one page.
- Both pages: form QR top right ("Scan to practise" + short address); a QR on every reference card with its `/go/` address printed under it.
- Footer on every page: `Printed from visualtkd.com/<slug> · <form name>` | `Edition <Month Year> · Page x of y`.
- The build decodes every QR from the rendered PDF; never ship a sheet that fails this check.

## Page behaviour

- Desktop: floor plan, study panel with Play, sequence table, references.
- Phone (≤760px): move-by-move carousel with minimap, plus a Floor plan toggle. No horizontal page scroll.
- Light and dark themes via CSS tokens; fonts Noto Serif KR (display/Korean), IBM Plex Sans Condensed (body), IBM Plex Mono (labels).

## Writing

Plain, direct English. Korean names romanised as Kukkiwon does (Taegeuk Il Jang, ap seogi, arae makki) with Hangul alongside. Describe meanings in your own words; don't copy Kukkiwon text.

## Deploy

AWS S3 + CloudFront via `python3 tools/deploy_aws.py` (stack `infra/aws/site.yaml`). The `/go/` short links are compiled from `site/_redirects` into the CloudFront function, so after changing any `refs` run the full deploy, not `--content`. Keep the directory-style URLs: the function redirects `/taegeuk/1` to `/taegeuk/1/` so each page's relative PDF link resolves.
